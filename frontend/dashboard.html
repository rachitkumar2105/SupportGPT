ℹ️ About SupportGPT

🚀 What We Do

SupportGPT is an AI-first support platform that transforms how businesses handle customer queries. We use advanced LLMs (Large Language Models) to provide instant, accurate answers by learning directly from your uploaded documents and past resolutions.

📖 How to Use

  • Upload: Go to 'Upload Docs' and add your invoices, PDFs, or guides.
  • Chat: Use 'AI Chat' to ask questions about your documents.
  • Earn: Get points for every interaction and climb the leaderboard!

🛠️ Resolve Queries

If the AI can't help, simply ask to "Create a ticket". Our Developer Team will review your issue, provide a custom solution, and you'll be notified automatically.
