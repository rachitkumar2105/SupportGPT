# Mobile apps and offline access

## Offline notes

On mobile, recently opened notes are cached automatically. To guarantee a whole notebook is available without a connection, open the notebook menu and choose Make available offline. Offline notebooks are available on Plus and Team; on Free, only recently opened notes are cached.

You can create and edit notes offline on every plan. Changes sync when the device reconnects.

## Battery and background sync

Battery saver modes on iOS and Android can pause background sync. If notes look out of date, open the app once to trigger a sync, or exclude Fieldnote from battery optimisation in the phone's settings.

## Widgets and sharing

Add the Quick note widget to your home screen to start a note in one tap. Use the system share sheet to save links, images and text from other apps straight into a notebook.

## Signing in on a new phone

Install the app and sign in with the same account. If you use 2FA, have your authenticator app or recovery codes ready. Moving to a new phone counts as a new device on the Free plan, so remove the old phone under Settings > Devices if you hit the 2 device limit.

## App versions

The mobile apps need iOS 16 or Android 10 or later. Phones on older versions keep the last compatible app version, but it no longer receives updates.
