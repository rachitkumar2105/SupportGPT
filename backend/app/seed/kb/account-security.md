# Account security

## Resetting your password

On the sign-in page choose "Forgot password" and enter your email address. The reset link is valid for 60 minutes and can be used once. Resetting your password signs you out of every other device. If you signed up with Google, you don't have a Fieldnote password; use "Continue with Google" instead.

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long. We check new passwords against lists of passwords exposed in known breaches and reject any that appear there.

## Two-factor authentication

You can turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) under Settings, then Security. Fieldnote supports authenticator apps (TOTP) and hardware security keys (WebAuthn/FIDO2). SMS codes are not supported because they can be intercepted through SIM-swap attacks.

When you enable 2FA we show ten single-use recovery codes. Store them somewhere safe, such as a password manager. Each code works once.

## Lost access to your 2FA device

If you lost your authenticator and still have a recovery code, sign in with your password and choose "Use a recovery code". If you have no recovery codes, contact support from the email address on the account. For your protection, identity verification for 2FA removal takes 3 business days and we notify the account email when the process starts, so a real owner can stop a fraudulent request.

## Account locked after failed sign-ins

After 10 failed sign-in attempts in a row, sign-in is paused for 30 minutes. Waiting the 30 minutes or resetting your password removes the lock.

## Suspicious activity

Under Settings, then Security, the Sessions list shows every device signed in to your account, with its approximate location and last activity. Use "Sign out" next to any session you don't recognise, then change your password and turn on 2FA. If you believe someone accessed your notes, contact support and mark the request urgent.

## Changing your email address

Change the account email under Settings, then Account. We send a confirmation link to the new address and a notice to the old address. The change only completes after you click the link in the new mailbox, which must happen within 24 hours.
