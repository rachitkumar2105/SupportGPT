# Refunds and cancellation

## Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel at any time under Settings, then Billing, then Cancel subscription. Cancelling stops future renewals. Your paid features stay active until the end of the period you already paid for, and then the account moves to the Free plan. Your notes are not deleted when you cancel.

On iOS, subscriptions bought through the App Store must be cancelled in your Apple ID subscription settings; Fieldnote can't cancel App Store subscriptions on your behalf. The same applies to Google Play subscriptions bought on Android.

## Refund policy

Monthly plans: we refund the most recent monthly payment if you ask within 14 days of being charged and haven't used more than 1 GB of new attachment storage in that period.

Annual plans: we give a full refund within 30 days of the initial purchase or of an annual renewal. After 30 days, annual plans are not refundable, but you can cancel to stop the next renewal.

Team plans: removing seats mid-term creates account credit for the unused time rather than a cash refund. Credit is applied automatically to the next invoice.

## Duplicate or unexpected charges

If you see two charges for the same period, the second is usually a pending authorisation that your bank releases within 5 business days. If both charges have settled, contact support with the last four digits of the card and the charge dates, and we will refund the duplicate in full. Never send your full card number to support.

## How long refunds take

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 2 business days. Your bank or card issuer may take a further 5 to 10 business days to show the money in your account. PayPal refunds usually appear within 1 business day. Refunds for App Store and Google Play purchases are handled by Apple and Google, not by Fieldnote.
