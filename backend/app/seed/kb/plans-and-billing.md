# Plans and billing

Fieldnote has three plans: Free, Plus and Team. Prices below are in US dollars and exclude local sales tax or VAT, which is added at checkout where it applies.

## Free plan

The Free plan costs nothing and includes unlimited notes, sync on up to two devices, 100 MB of attachment storage, and 7 days of version history. Free accounts cannot publish notes to the web.

## Plus plan

Plus costs $6 per month, or $60 per year when billed annually (two months free). It includes sync on unlimited devices, 10 GB of attachment storage, 1 year of version history, publishing notes to the web, and offline notebooks on mobile.

## Team plan

Team costs $10 per user per month, or $96 per user per year when billed annually. It includes everything in Plus, 50 GB of pooled storage per team, shared notebooks with role-based permissions, single sign-on (SAML) and an admin console. Team workspaces need a minimum of 3 seats.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. Annual Team plans of 20 seats or more can also pay by bank transfer against an invoice with 30-day terms. We do not accept cryptocurrency or prepaid gift cards.

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and you are charged a prorated amount for the rest of the current billing period. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period, so you keep paid features until then. If you downgrade from Plus to Free and your attachments exceed 100 MB, existing attachments stay readable but you can't upload new ones until you are under the limit.

## Invoices and receipts

Every payment generates a receipt that is emailed to the billing contact. You can download past invoices as PDFs under Settings, then Billing, then Invoice history. To add a company name, address or VAT number to future invoices, edit the billing profile on the same page. Invoices that were already issued cannot be changed, but we can reissue an invoice for the last 90 days if you contact support.

## Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, we retry it three times over 7 days and email the billing contact after each failed attempt. After the final failed retry the account moves to the Free plan. No notes are deleted when this happens; paid features return as soon as a successful payment is made.
