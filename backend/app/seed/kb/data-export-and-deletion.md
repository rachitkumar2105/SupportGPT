# Data export, backups and account deletion

## Exporting your notes

You can export everything under Settings, then Data, then Export. Choose Markdown (a .zip of .md files with an attachments folder, preserving the notebook structure) or JSON (one file including tags, timestamps and version metadata). Exports up to 1 GB are ready within a few minutes; larger exports can take up to 24 hours and we email you a download link that stays valid for 7 days.

To export a single note, open the note menu and choose "Export as Markdown" or "Export as PDF".

## Version history and the trash

Every note keeps a version history: 7 days on Free and 1 year on Plus and Team. Open a note, choose "History", pick a version and select "Restore" to bring it back.

Deleted notes go to the trash and stay there for 30 days before they are permanently removed. You can restore a note from the trash at any time within those 30 days. Emptying the trash deletes notes permanently and they cannot be recovered by support.

## Importing from other apps

Fieldnote imports Evernote .enex files, plain Markdown folders, and Notion exports in Markdown format. Imports are under Settings, then Data, then Import. Notion databases are imported as tables inside notes; relations and rollups are not preserved.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account under Settings, then Account, then Delete account. If you have a paid subscription, cancel it first. After you confirm, the account is deactivated immediately and permanently deleted after 14 days. During those 14 days you can sign in to cancel the deletion. After 14 days your notes, attachments and account data are erased from our production systems, and from encrypted backups within a further 35 days.

## Data location and privacy

Notes are stored in data centres in the European Union (Frankfurt) by default. Team plans can choose US storage when the workspace is created; the region can't be changed afterwards. Notes are encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Fieldnote staff can't read your notes unless you grant temporary access for a support request.
