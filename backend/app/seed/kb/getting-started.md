# Getting started with Fieldnote

Fieldnote is a note-taking app for individuals and small teams. Notes are written in Markdown, organised into notebooks, and synced across the web app, the desktop apps for macOS and Windows, and the mobile apps for iOS and Android.

## Creating your account

You can sign up with an email address and password, or with a Google account. After signing up we send a verification link that stays valid for 24 hours. Until your email is verified you can create notes, but sharing and publishing are disabled.

If the verification email doesn't arrive within ten minutes, check your spam folder, then use "Resend verification email" on the Account page. Work email systems sometimes quarantine messages from new senders, so ask your IT team to allow mail from no-reply@fieldnote.example.

## Notebooks, notes and tags

A notebook is a folder for notes. You can nest notebooks up to three levels deep. Each note belongs to exactly one notebook but can carry any number of tags. Tags are shared across all notebooks, which makes them the right tool for topics that cut across projects, such as "meeting" or "follow-up".

To move a note, drag it onto another notebook in the sidebar, or open the note menu and choose "Move to". Moving a note keeps its tags, attachments and version history.

## Keyboard shortcuts

On desktop and web, press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on macOS) to open quick search, Ctrl+N for a new note, and Ctrl+Shift+F to search inside the current notebook. The full shortcut list is under Help, then Keyboard shortcuts.

## Supported platforms

The desktop apps support macOS 12 or later and Windows 10 or later. The mobile apps support iOS 16 or later and Android 10 or later. The web app works in the current and previous major versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. Linux is supported through the web app only; there is no native Linux desktop app.
